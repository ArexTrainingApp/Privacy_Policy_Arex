# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Arex** handles your information.

---

## Summary

Arex is designed to work entirely on your device.

**Arex does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on external servers.** Arex has no backend, no user accounts, and does not require you to sign in.

Your workouts, workout history, points, streaks, achievements, and other app data are stored locally on your device and are not accessible to Arex or third parties.

---

## Information We Collect

**Arex does not collect any personal data.**

The app does not collect:

- Personal information
- Usage data
- Device identifiers
- Analytics
- Advertising or tracking data

Arex does not require an account or sign-in, and no personal data is transmitted from the app to our servers.

---

## Data Storage

All app data, including:

- Workouts you create
- Workout history
- Points
- Streaks
- Achievements
- Other app-related data

is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms.

**This data does not leave your device and is not accessible to Arex or any third party.**

---

## Third-Party Services

Arex does not integrate third-party:

- Analytics SDKs
- Advertising SDKs
- Tracking SDKs
- Data collection services

Arex does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties.

---

## Data Security

Because Arex does not transmit or store user data on external servers, there is no remote database containing your personal information.

Data stored locally on your device is protected by the security mechanisms provided by iOS.

---

## Children's Privacy

Arex does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because Arex does not collect personal information from its users.

---

## Deleting Your Data

You can delete all app data at any time from within the app by going to:

**Settings → Reset All Data**

This permanently deletes your:

- Workouts
- Workout history
- Points
- Streaks
- Achievements
- Other locally stored app data

from your device.

Uninstalling Arex also removes the app and its locally stored data from your device, subject to standard iOS behavior regarding backups.

---

If this Privacy Policy changes in the future, we will update this page and the **Last updated** date before the changes take effect.

For example, if a future version of Arex introduces an optional cloud sync feature, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe how that feature handles data.
